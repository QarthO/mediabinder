# MediaBinder branding

`MediaBinder.af` is the editable Affinity source; `board.png` is the review board.
The app/profile symbols are centered by the visible artwork bounds, including
rings, rather than the original SVG viewport. The primary lockup has no motto.

The shared `public/favicon.svg` is the primary blue binder/play mark, used for
both the app header and browser icon. Its viewport centers the visible artwork.
Keep its cutouts transparent and its fill blue; do not substitute a profile tile.

| Color       | Value     | App role                             |
| ----------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Binder blue | `#397CF6` | Primary symbol                       |
| Sky         | `#78ACFA` | Interactive accents on dark surfaces |
| Ink         | `#111A2B` | Reverse artwork                      |
| Ice         | `#EAF2FF` | Foreground text                      |
| White       | `#FFFFFF` | Reverse artwork                      |

The app keeps its original charcoal background (`#111316`) and neutral panels,
with blue reserved for branding and interaction accents. Geist is the existing app typeface;
the wordmark uses Media at 700 and Binder at 450. The board's lettering is outlined
for portability. Google consent-screen branding is updated separately after review.
